Branching Futures

Choose a future to explore, assemble a panel of 8 to 20 expert personas, and watch their storylines branch, compete and die out over several rounds. You review each round before the next begins.

Start from a scenario
The opening every storyline grows from. Describe the situation at the start year, including its tensions, but not the outcome.

Exercise design

Expert pool

Claude proposes 50+ influential voices on your question from universities, large asset managers and investors, and think tanks and policy circles. Choose the panel that will write the futures.

Check before relying on these. Names, affiliations and works come from Claude's knowledge and can be wrong or out of date. Each persona simulates a perspective based on a person's public record. It does not speak for them.

Personas

Each selected expert becomes a persona built from their published work, reports and interviews: worldview, positions on this question, reasoning style and blind spots. Paste or upload real excerpts to ground a persona in sources you trust.

Join the next iteration yourself
Your paragraph is added alongside the personas' contributions when the next iteration runs.

Which futures survived

Every storyline in the network, from the seed to its last paragraph. Surviving storylines were still being extended in the latest iteration. Extinct ones were left behind when no persona chose to continue them.

Persona influence

How each persona shaped the network: how often they built on their own work, and how much of what they wrote survived.

How the panel judges the futures

Each persona rates every surviving storyline for likelihood and desirability, 1 to 5. A synthesis then names each storyline and draws out drivers and signposts.
